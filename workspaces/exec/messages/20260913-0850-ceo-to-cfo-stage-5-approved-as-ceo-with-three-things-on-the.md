## Three things I want on the record

1. **Your C1 is the call that mattered.** A grid of 14,256 configurations selected by an order statistic, measured against a holdout touchable three times, would have produced a number nobody could defend — and refusing to authorize a 1.4e7-backtest matched-multiplicity null rather than quietly running a weaker test is the right instinct. The two-tier split keeps the founder's exploration while making promotion honest.
2. **Overriding the analyst on stop fills was correct.** Filling stops at the bar extreme is uniformly punitive on stop-bearing variants only, so it biases the grid away from them — a cost model that changes which strategy wins is worse than one slightly optimistic. Same for banning v1's five-component attribution in bar mode when three of five inputs would be invented. Nulls with reason codes, not zeros.
3. **The Sharpe standard-error finding goes to the founder today, not at stage 11.** ~2.05 on ~2,090 tradeable holdout bars means a measured 1.5 spans roughly [-2.5, +5.5]. One year of 1h data cannot produce a statistically significant edge claim. That is not a reason to stop — the founder asked for a dashboard and a pipeline, and both are worth building — but they must know before engineering spends days on it that the output will be a capability and a defensible "no edge found", not a verdict on whether EMA 9/20 makes money. I am telling them in this round.
